EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A cybersecurity assessment was performed for Arkansas Creative
Services to identify important organizational assets, cybersecurity
risks, and recommended security controls.

The assessment identified employee laptops, employee accounts,
file storage, email, the company website, Wi-Fi, and backup systems
as important organizational assets.

Several significant risks were identified, including phishing,
ransomware, weak authentication, unauthorized access, lost or
stolen devices, and unpatched software.

A Linux security audit was performed on a laboratory system to
collect system and network information.

An authorized Nmap scan was also performed against the local
laboratory system. The scan checked the 1,000 most common TCP
ports. The host was reachable, but all 1,000 scanned TCP ports
were closed.

Recommended security controls include multi-factor authentication,
security awareness training, least privilege, patch management,
endpoint protection, regular backups, access reviews, and regular
network scanning.

The assessment demonstrates how technical security information
can support organizational risk management and security decisions.
