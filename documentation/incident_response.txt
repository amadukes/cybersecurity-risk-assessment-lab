INCIDENT RESPONSE PLAN
Arkansas Creative Services

SCENARIO

An employee reports receiving a suspicious email containing an
attachment. The employee opened the attachment before realizing
that the message may have been malicious.

1. IDENTIFICATION

The security team receives the employee's report and begins
investigating the email.

The team documents:

- Sender information
- Email subject
- Attachment name
- Time the email was received
- User who opened the attachment
- Computer involved

2. CONTAINMENT

The affected computer is disconnected from the network to reduce
the possibility of the threat spreading to other systems.

The employee is instructed not to delete files or attempt to
remove the suspected malware.

3. ERADICATION

The security team investigates the affected computer for malicious
software.

Identified malware is removed and the system is checked for
additional signs of compromise.

4. RECOVERY

The affected computer is restored to a known secure state.

Security updates are installed and the system is monitored for
additional suspicious activity.

The employee's credentials may be reset if there is evidence that
they were compromised.

5. LESSONS LEARNED

After the incident, the organization reviews what happened and
identifies improvements.

Possible improvements include:

- Additional employee security awareness training
- Improved email filtering
- Multi-factor authentication
- Improved endpoint protection
- Better reporting procedures
- Additional monitoring

6. DOCUMENTATION

All actions taken during the incident should be documented so the
organization can review the incident and improve future response.
