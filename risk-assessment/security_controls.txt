SECURITY CONTROLS MATRIX
Arkansas Creative Services

RISK: PHISHING

Threat:
Employees receive malicious emails.

Recommended Controls:
- Security awareness training
- Email filtering
- Multi-factor authentication
- Phishing simulations

Purpose:
Reduce the likelihood of successful phishing attacks and limit
damage if an employee's credentials are compromised.


RISK: RANSOMWARE

Threat:
Malware encrypts company files.

Recommended Controls:
- Regular backups
- Endpoint protection
- Patch management
- Least privilege
- Network segmentation

Purpose:
Reduce the likelihood of infection and improve recovery after
a ransomware incident.


RISK: WEAK PASSWORDS

Threat:
Attackers obtain access by guessing or cracking passwords.

Recommended Controls:
- Strong password requirements
- Multi-factor authentication
- Account lockout
- Password manager

Purpose:
Make unauthorized account access more difficult.


RISK: UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS

Threat:
Users access information they do not need for their job.

Recommended Controls:
- Least privilege
- Role-based access control
- Access reviews
- File permissions

Purpose:
Limit access to sensitive information.


RISK: LOST OR STOLEN LAPTOP

Threat:
A laptop containing company information is lost or stolen.

Recommended Controls:
- Full-disk encryption
- Strong authentication
- Automatic screen locking
- Remote device management

Purpose:
Reduce the possibility of sensitive information being exposed.


RISK: UNPATCHED SOFTWARE

Threat:
Attackers exploit known vulnerabilities in outdated software.

Recommended Controls:
- Patch management
- Vulnerability scanning
- Software inventory
- Endpoint monitoring

Purpose:
Reduce exposure to known software vulnerabilities.
