RISK REGISTER
Arkansas Creative Services

Risk 1: Phishing Attack
Asset: Employee Accounts
Threat: Attacker sends a fraudulent email to an employee.
Likelihood: High
Impact: High
Risk Level: High

Controls:
- Security awareness training
- Multi-factor authentication
- Email filtering
- Phishing simulations

Risk 2: Ransomware
Asset: File Server
Threat: Malware encrypts company files.
Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High
Risk Level: High

Controls:
- Regular backups
- Endpoint protection
- Least privilege
- Patch management
- Network segmentation

Risk 3: Weak Passwords
Asset: Employee Accounts
Threat: Attacker guesses or cracks employee passwords.
Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High
Risk Level: High

Controls:
- Strong password requirements
- Multi-factor authentication
- Account lockout
- Password manager

Risk 4: Unauthorized Access
Asset: File Server
Threat: Employee or attacker accesses files without authorization.
Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High
Risk Level: High

Controls:
- Role-based access control
- Least privilege
- Access reviews
- File permissions

Risk 5: Lost or Stolen Laptop
Asset: Employee Laptop
Threat: Physical loss exposes company information.
Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High
Risk Level: High

Controls:
- Full-disk encryption
- Strong authentication
- Remote device management
- Automatic screen locking

Risk 6: Unpatched Software
Asset: Company Computers
Threat: Attackers exploit known vulnerabilities.
Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High
Risk Level: High

Controls:
- Patch management
- Vulnerability scanning
- Software inventory
- Endpoint monitoring
